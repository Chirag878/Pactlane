# Ideas

A parking lot. These are opportunities, not implementation commitments. Move an idea to [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) only when it has a concrete reason to be built.

## Creative experiments and interaction concepts

- **Visual Commitment Trail.** A horizontal thread through meetings, with each meeting a stop and the commitment's state changing along the way.
- **Commitment health indicators.** A calm signal per commitment based on age, delays and how recently it was discussed.
- **Intelligent next-meeting agenda.** Open, overdue and repeatedly delayed items, ordered by what most needs discussion.
- **Team accountability patterns.** Trends such as a type of work that often slips, framed around process and not blame.
- **Delayed-work explanations.** "Why is this late?" answered from quotes across meetings.
- **Agent-generated resolution proposals.** Split the work, move the date or reassign, each with its reasoning.
- **Natural-language activity exploration.** "What changed since Tuesday?" or "What has Priya promised this month?"
- **Fact, proposal and action styling.** A consistent visual language so people instantly know which is which.
- **Prompt-injection moment.** A transcript line tries to reassign everything and the agent flags it instead of obeying.

## Product

- Commitments that depend on other commitments.
- Confidence labels explaining why an item needs review.
- A weekly digest of commitments due and at risk.
- Personal view: "My commitments across all projects."

## Integrations

- Slack or Teams follow-up delivery, with approval.
- Meeting-recorder transcript import.

## Intelligence

- Automatic alias learning, such as "Rav" being Aarav.
